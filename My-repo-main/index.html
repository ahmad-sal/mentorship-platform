The process

How the mentorship works

A clear path from choosing a skill to having your work reviewed.

1

Choose your track

Browse the course tracks and enrol in the skill that matches your goals. Your workspace is created the moment you enrol.

2

Work through the lessons

Follow each curriculum question, watch the reference lesson where one is attached, and mark tasks complete as you go.

3

Submit for mentor review

Attach your final practical work and send it in. Your mentor reviews the submission and returns feedback in your workspace.

Your workspace

A learning workspace, not a video dump

Everything you submit and complete lives in one place, tied to your account.

Student dashboard

Pick a track, see your enrolled courses, and open the active course workspace without leaving the page.

Curriculum tasks & questions

Each track lists numbered questions. Locked tasks unlock in order so you are never asked to skip ahead.

Reference video lessons

Where a question includes a video requirement, your answer field unlocks once the study time is fulfilled.

Real-time progress

Track completed tasks and overall percentage per course on your dashboard and student profile.

Assignment submissions

Attach documents, images or archives — PDF, DOCX, ZIP, PNG, JPG and PPTX are supported up to 10 MB.

Private student account

Access is protected by your own student login. Only you and your mentor can see your submissions.
